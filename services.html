Medical Cupping Therapy (Hijama)

Hijama, or medical cupping therapy, is an ancient healing practice that has been used for centuries across many cultures. At Para Errahali, our certified hijama specialist performs this therapy in a clean, clinical environment using sterilized equipment.

The therapy involves placing specialized cups on the skin to create suction, which promotes blood flow, relieves muscle tension, and supports the body's natural healing processes.

Benefits of Hijama

  • Pain Relief — Effective for back pain, neck pain, migraines, and joint discomfort
  • Improved Blood Circulation — Enhances oxygen flow and nutrient delivery to tissues
  • Detoxification — Helps remove toxins and waste from the body
  • Stress & Anxiety Relief — Promotes deep relaxation and mental well-being
  • Skin Health — Can help with acne, eczema, and cellulite reduction
  • Immune System Support — Strengthens the body's natural defenses
